# Case A: Business permit applications

Label: "citizen services in the cloud"

- A city administration receives about 40,000 business permit applications a year through an online form.
- Applicants upload company documents and identity proofs. Clerks review each application in a case management system.
- Decisions are legally binding and must stay retrievable for ten years.
- The service exchanges data with the national business register and the payment provider.
- Applications arrive evenly through the year. An outage of a few hours delays decisions but loses no rights.

Not supplied: which records law applies, recovery targets, budget.
