# Case B: Public transport journey planner

Label: "citizen services in the cloud"

- A regional transport authority offers a journey planner as a website and an open API.
- Timetables and stop data are public open data. The planner does not need an account.
- Usage peaks on weekday mornings and grows tenfold during disruptions and large events.
- A route calculation must answer within two seconds, otherwise people switch to other apps.
- Live vehicle positions arrive every ten seconds from the operators' fleet systems.

Not supplied: whether request logs contain location data and how long they are kept, budget.
